Problem Statement: CAMPTOCORP - AI Powered 
Campus-to-Corporate Placement Management & 
Analytics Platform 
Domain: EdTech + Artificial Intelligence / Placement & Career Services Automation 
Background 
College and university placement cells are responsible for managing thousands of students, 
hundreds of recruiters and dozens of overlapping placement drives every academic season. 
Most placement cells still rely on scattered spreadsheets, email chains, WhatsApp groups 
and manual shortlisting to run this process, resulting in duplicated effort, missed deadlines 
and inconsistent communication. 
Students often have no single re adiness profile, skill-gap analysis or personalised guidance 
on which recruiters or roles they best fit, while recruiters struggle to receive well -matched, 
pre-screened candidate pools. 
Placement drives frequently clash in scheduling, offer letters and joining statuses are tracked 
manually, and placement cells have little to no analytics on which branches, skills or 
preparation efforts actually convert into offers. 
There is therefore a need for an AI -powered, end-to-end Campus-to-Corporate Placement 
Management System that can digitise, automate and intelligently optimise the entire 
placement lifecycle. 
Challenge 
Design and develop an AI -powered Campus -to-Corporate Placement Management 
System capable of managing student readiness, matching students to recruit er 
requirements, scheduling placement drives and generating actionable placement 
analytics in near real time. 
The proposed system should use technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine 
Learning, Natural Language Processing, recommendation systems  or Generative AI to 
analyse student profiles, recruiter requirements and historical placement data to streamline 
the placement process. 
The system should not merely digitise records. It should also provide: 
● Student readiness and employability scoring 
● Skill-gap analysis against target job roles 
● Recruiter-student matching and ranking 
● Conflict-free drive scheduling 
● Offer, documentation and joining-status tracking 
● Predictive analytics on placement outcomes

Key Placement Management Areas 
Teams may address a combination of the following: 
1. Student Readiness Profiling 
Build a comprehensive, continuously updated profile for every student covering: 
● Resume and academic records 
● Certifications and projects 
● Aptitude and mock-interview scores 
● Communication and soft-skill assessments 
● Branch, CGPA and backlog history 
The system should identify indicators such as skill gaps, low readiness scores and areas 
needing improvement before a student is put forward to a recruiter. 
2. Recruiter Requirement Matching 
Parse recruiter job descriptions and eligibility criteria, then i dentify or assess the best -fit 
candidates by: 
● Skill and technology-stack matching 
● Branch and CGPA eligibility filtering 
● Certification and project relevance 
● Past interview performance 
The system may generate a fit/match score and highlight the reasons a student was or was 
not shortlisted. 
3. Drive Scheduling & Conflict Management 
Detect and resolve scheduling issues such as: 
● Overlapping company drives on the same date/slot 
● Venue and resource double-booking 
● Students shortlisted for multiple simultaneous drives 
● Interview-panel and infrastructure availability 
4. Offer & Documentation Tracking 
Track the complete post-selection lifecycle, including: 
● Offer letters and CTC details 
● Joining bonds, documentation and verification status 
● PPOs (pre-placement offers) and internship conversions 
● Offer acceptance, deferral or withdrawal 
5. Analytics & Predictive Insights 
Generate insights such as: 
● Branch-wise and skill-wise placement conversion rates

● Salary and package trends across recruiters 
● Students at risk of remaining unplaced 
● Recruiter engagement and repeat-hiring patterns 
6. Communication & Notification Automation 
Replace manual email/WhatsApp coordination with automated, targeted notifications for: 
● Shortlisting and interview schedules 
● Document submission deadlines 
● Offer status updates 
● Drive announcements and eligibility criteria 
Expected Solution 
The proposed prototype should preferably contain the following modules: 
A. Multi-Source Data Integration Engine 
The system may integrate and analyse: 
● Student academic and resume data 
● Recruiter job descriptions and eligibility criteria 
● Placement drive calendars 
● Historical placement records 
● Assessment and mock-interview results 
B. AI-Based Matching & Scoring Engine 
Participants may use: 
● Machine Learning 
● Natural Language Processing 
● Recommendation Systems 
● Large Language Models 
● Predictive Analytics 
● Rule + AI Hybrid Models 
C. Readiness & Fit Scoring 
Every student-recruiter pairing should be assigned an appropriate readiness/fit level, for 
example: 
Not Ready → Developing → Ready → Highly Employable 
The system should explain the major factors behind the score. 
D. Explainable Recommendations 
Instead of providing only:
